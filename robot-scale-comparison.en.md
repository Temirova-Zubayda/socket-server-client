**Where the Pinky number comes from:**
- Piaggio gita, a person-following cargo robot, follows at 3–5 ft, which is 0.9–1.5 m ([gita Quick Facts](https://cdn.mediactive-network.net/events/vivatech/uploads/services/surcouche/gita-Business-Quick-Facts_v1.2_1621959011_UMH9PU.pdf)).
- One research robot that follows people used a following distance of 1.3–1.5 m ([PMC11644850](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11644850)).
- In a user study comparing 1.2 m and 2.0 m, most users preferred 2.0 m ([Siebert et al., 2020](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2020.00074/pdf)).
- Hall's personal zone is 0.45–1.2 m ([Pacchierotti et al., 2006](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~hic/hic-papers/roman06-pacchierotti-final.pdf)).
